# v1.0.1 — two fixes

### الكابتن ماجد ٤ بالعربية

A point release on top of v1.0. Same translation, two bugs out.

## What changed

**The 1-2 read «ينفذ الكرة!».** The commentator picks his verb from the
action, and the 1-2 (ワンツー) had been given the generic «ينفذ» that goes in
front of a special move. It now has its own verb: «ماجد يبادل الكرة!».
Named combinations — الثنائي الذهبي، هجوم الجوزاء… — still read «ينفذ».

**The pause menu still said سجل.** The game has two pause boxes, and only one
of them had been changed to راقب. Both say راقب now.
